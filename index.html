Ciao, I'm Pietro.

I was born and raised in Italy and lived there for 19 years. For more than two years now I've been studying at a university in the United States, so Italian is my native language and I speak English fluently every day.

That's what makes my lessons different. I know the Italian people really speak at the bar, on the train and around the dinner table, and I've been through learning a second language myself. Questions

I've never studied Italian. Can I start with you?

Of course. Beginners are very welcome. I explain everything in English at first and bring in more Italian as you get comfortable.

How often should I have lessons?

Most students do one or two 60-minute lessons a week. We'll decide together on the intro call based on your goals and schedule.

What do I need for a lesson?

A computer or tablet with a camera and microphone and a stable internet connection. I send everything else.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes, for free with at least 24 hours' notice. Lessons cancelled later, or missed, are charged in full.

Do the package hours expire?

The 5-hour package is valid for 2 months and the 10-hour package for 4 months from purchase.
